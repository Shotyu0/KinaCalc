# Kina Ledger

A crafting profit calculator for AION 2 (Global), made for a small group of friends.

- Ranks every craft by expected profit per attempt. It compares buying the materials, gathering them yourself, and selling what you gather instead.
- Success chances by item grade and crafting level, read from the in-game Crafting Chance tables.
- Splendent combo procs, with the cost of chasing one and whether to sell it or craft the next tier with it.
- A shared price book with price history. Price lists can be pasted straight from a market site.

The shared price book is invite-only.
